# Region Summary: Pineal Body

**Region name:** pineal body
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** epithalamus
**Latin / meaning:** "pine-cone"
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The pineal body is the brain's melatonin-secreting endocrine gland and circadian signaller. It is responsible for secretion of melatonin under circadian control, signalling night and timing sleep and seasonal rhythms.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Secrete melatonin.** Release melatonin at night under suprachiasmatic (circadian) control, promoting sleep onset.
- **Signal time and season.** Encode day length, influencing circadian and seasonal physiology.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Pineal dysfunction disturbs sleep timing and melatonin rhythm; it commonly calcifies with age (a radiological landmark).

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a clock-driven hormone output: a melatonin emitter gated by the circadian system.
