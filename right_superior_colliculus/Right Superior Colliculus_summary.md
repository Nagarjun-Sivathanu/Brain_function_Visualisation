# Region Summary: Right Superior Colliculus

**Region name:** right superior colliculus
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** right side of midbrain
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The right superior colliculus is the right midbrain's visual-reflex and gaze-orienting centre. It is responsible for reflexive orienting of the eyes and head toward salient stimuli in the right tectum, on a retinotopic map.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Orient gaze.** Command saccades and head turns that point the fovea at a salient visual (and multisensory) target.
- **Hold a spatial map.** Maintain a retinotopic/spatial map that aligns visual, auditory, and somatosensory space.
- **Drive visual reflexes.** Mediate fast, automatic orienting and avoidance to sudden stimuli.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage impairs reflexive orienting and saccade generation toward the contralateral field; it underlies 'blindsight'-type residual orienting when cortex is lost.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a 'where + look here' reflex node: it builds a salience map of space and triggers gaze toward the winner.
