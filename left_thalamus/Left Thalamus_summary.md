# Region Summary: Left Thalamus

**Region name:** left thalamus
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** thalamus
**Major components:** left ventral nuclear group, left lateral nuclear group, left anterior nucleus, left dorsomedial nucleus, left intralaminar nuclei

---

## 1. Overview

The left thalamus is the left half of the thalamus — sensory/motor relay and arousal gateway for the right body. It is responsible for relay and gating of right-body sensation and motor signals to the left cortex, plus arousal and attention.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Relay the opposite body.** Synapse and forward right-sided somatosensory, visual, and auditory signals to the left cortex.
- **Relay motor signals.** Pass cerebellar and basal-ganglia output to the ipsilateral motor cortex.
- **Gate arousal/attention.** Filter what reaches cortex and help set wakefulness via thalamocortical loops.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage to the left thalamus causes right-sided sensory loss and central pain, with neglect or aphasia depending on side.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the left relay/gate: the labelled-line router for the right body and motor loops into the left cortex.
