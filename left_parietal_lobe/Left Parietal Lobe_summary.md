# Region Summary: Left Parietal Lobe

**Region name:** left parietal lobe
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** left cerebral hemisphere
**Major components:** postcentral gyrus (primary somatosensory cortex), superior parietal lobule, inferior parietal lobule (supramarginal & angular gyri), intraparietal sulcus, precuneus

---

## 1. Overview

The left parietal lobe is the left somatosensory and spatial-integration lobe. It is responsible for touch, proprioception, and pain from the right body (primary somatosensory cortex), and spatial awareness, attention, and sensorimotor integration.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Perceive body sensation.** The postcentral gyrus maps touch, temperature, pain, and proprioception from the right side of the body.
- **Build spatial awareness.** Integrate vision, touch, and proprioception into a body- and world-centred map for reaching and navigation.
- **Direct attention.** Allocate spatial attention. It supports reading, calculation, and praxis.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage causes right-sided sensory loss, apraxia, and — on the right — hemispatial neglect; left inferior-parietal damage impairs reading, writing, and calculation (Gerstmann features).

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the 'where + body' integrator: it fuses senses into spatial maps and steers attention and reaching.
