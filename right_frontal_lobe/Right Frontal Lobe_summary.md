# Region Summary: Right Frontal Lobe

**Region name:** right frontal lobe
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** right cerebral hemisphere
**Major components:** precentral gyrus (primary motor cortex), premotor & supplementary motor areas, prefrontal cortex, inferior frontal gyrus, frontal eye fields, orbitofrontal cortex

---

## 1. Overview

The right frontal lobe is the right executive and motor lobe — voluntary movement, planning, decisions, and personality. It is responsible for voluntary movement of the left body (primary motor cortex), executive function, working memory, decision-making, social/emotional regulation, and speech.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Command voluntary movement.** The precentral gyrus issues the motor commands that move the left side of the body; premotor/SMA plan and sequence them.
- **Run executive function.** Prefrontal cortex handles planning, working memory, reasoning, attention control, and goal-directed behaviour.
- **Regulate behaviour and emotion.** Orbitofrontal/ventromedial cortex governs impulse control, social judgement, and value-based decisions.
- **Support language and gaze.** It contributes prosody and emotional expression to speech. The frontal eye fields direct voluntary gaze.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Frontal damage causes left-sided weakness, disorganised planning, perseveration, personality/impulse change (cf. Phineas Gage), and — on the left — Broca's (expressive) aphasia.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the brain's executive + motor commander: it sets goals, plans and sequences actions, gates impulses, and issues movement.
