# Region Summary: Brachia of Right Superior Colliculus

**Region name:** brachia of right superior colliculus
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** right side of midbrain
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The brachia of right superior colliculus is the right white-matter arm carrying visual fibres to the superior colliculus and pretectum. It is responsible for conduction of retinal and cortical visual fibres into the right superior colliculus and pretectal area.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Deliver visual input.** Carry fibres from the optic tract/retina and visual cortex to the superior colliculus for orienting.
- **Serve reflex centres.** Route fibres to the pretectum for the pupillary light reflex.

## 3. Behavioural Description

As a fibre bundle it has no standalone function; damage interrupts the visual input to collicular orienting and pupillary reflexes.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a dedicated input cable feeding the visual-reflex centres.
