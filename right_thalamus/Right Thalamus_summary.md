# Region Summary: Right Thalamus

**Region name:** right thalamus
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** thalamus
**Major components:** right ventral nuclear group, right lateral nuclear group, right anterior nucleus, right dorsomedial nucleus, right intralaminar nuclei

---

## 1. Overview

The right thalamus is the right half of the thalamus — sensory/motor relay and arousal gateway for the left body. It is responsible for relay and gating of left-body sensation and motor signals to the right cortex, plus arousal and attention.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Relay the opposite body.** Synapse and forward left-sided somatosensory, visual, and auditory signals to the right cortex.
- **Relay motor signals.** Pass cerebellar and basal-ganglia output to the ipsilateral motor cortex.
- **Gate arousal/attention.** Filter what reaches cortex and help set wakefulness via thalamocortical loops.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage to the right thalamus causes left-sided sensory loss and central pain, with neglect or aphasia depending on side.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the right relay/gate: the labelled-line router for the left body and motor loops into the right cortex.
