# Region Summary: Right Cerebral Hemisphere

**Region name:** right cerebral hemisphere
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** telencephalon
**Major components:** right frontal lobe, right parietal lobe, right temporal lobe, right occipital lobe, right limbic lobe, right insula, subcortex of right cerebral hemisphere, right lateral ventricle

---

## 1. Overview

The right cerebral hemisphere is the right half of the cerebrum — conscious perception, voluntary action, and cognition for the left side of the body. It is responsible for the right cortical lobes, right basal ganglia and white matter, controlling sensation and movement of the left body, plus cognition, memory, and emotion.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Perceive and act for the opposite body.** Process sensation from and command movement of the left side of the body (the motor/sensory pathways cross).
- **Run cognition.** Carry out perception, attention, planning, decision-making, and spatial/visuospatial and prosodic processing across its lobes.
- **Hold memory and emotion.** Support declarative memory (temporal/limbic) and emotional processing (limbic, insula).
- **Coordinate via subcortex.** Use its basal ganglia to select and gate movement and habits.

## 3. Behavioural Description

The right hemisphere governs the left body and, by lateralisation, spatial attention and prosody. Damage causes left-sided weakness/sensory loss and hemineglect.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a full cortical+subcortical processor for one side: route the relevant lobe sub-agents and integrate their outputs into perception, decision, and action.
