# Region Summary: Left Red Nucleus

**Region name:** left red nucleus
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** left side of midbrain
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The left red nucleus is the left midbrain motor-relay nucleus of the rubrospinal system. It is responsible for coordination of left limb movement via the rubrospinal tract and the cerebello-rubro-thalamic loop.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Drive the rubrospinal tract.** Send crossed motor commands to the spinal cord that bias flexor tone, contributing to limb movement (more prominent in development and in some recovery).
- **Relay cerebellar output.** Pass cerebellar signals onward in the cerebello-rubro-thalamic loop for smooth, coordinated movement.
- **Aid motor coordination.** Help time and scale ongoing movements alongside the corticospinal system.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Lesions around the red nucleus disturb coordination and can produce tremor; classic decerebrate vs decorticate posturing depends on damage relative to it.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a motor relay that forwards cerebellar coordination signals to the cord and biases limb tone.
