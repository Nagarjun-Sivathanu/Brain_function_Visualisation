# Region Summary: Left Insula

**Region name:** left insula
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** left cerebral hemisphere
**Major components:** anterior insula, posterior insula, limen insulae

---

## 1. Overview

The left insula is the left interoceptive and salience cortex buried in the lateral sulcus. It is responsible for interoception (the felt state of the body), taste, pain and temperature affect, emotional awareness, and salience detection.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Sense the inner body.** Map interoceptive signals — heartbeat, breathing, gut, temperature, pain — into the felt bodily state.
- **Process taste and disgust.** Host primary gustatory cortex and the feeling of disgust.
- **Flag salience and emotion.** The anterior insula (with anterior cingulate) forms the salience network, detecting what matters and binding it to subjective emotion (e.g., empathy, craving).

## 3. Behavioural Description

Insular damage blunts interoceptive awareness, taste, and the emotional colouring of bodily and social experience; it is central to addiction craving and anxiety.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the interoceptive/salience hub: it reads the body's internal state, judges what is significant, and feeds subjective feeling.
