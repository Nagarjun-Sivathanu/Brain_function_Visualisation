# Region Summary: Left Optic Tract

**Region name:** left optic tract
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** diencephalon
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The left optic tract is the post-chiasmatic visual pathway carrying the right visual hemifield toward the left thalamus. It is responsible for transmission of the combined right visual hemifield (from both eyes) to the left lateral geniculate nucleus, pretectum, and superior colliculus.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Carry a hemifield.** After partial decussation at the chiasm, convey the right visual hemifield from both retinae to the left side.
- **Relay to the thalamus.** Project mainly to the left lateral geniculate nucleus for onward conscious vision.
- **Feed reflex centres.** Send collaterals to the pretectum (pupillary reflex) and superior colliculus (gaze/orienting).

## 3. Behavioural Description

A lesion of the left optic tract causes a contralateral (right) homonymous hemianopia affecting both eyes.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a hemifield conduit: a routed cable carrying one half of visual space to its thalamic and reflex targets.
