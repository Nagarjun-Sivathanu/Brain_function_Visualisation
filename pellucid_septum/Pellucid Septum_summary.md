# Region Summary: Pellucid Septum

**Region name:** pellucid septum
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** septum of telencephalon
**Latin / meaning:** "translucent partition"
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The pellucid septum is the thin midline membrane separating the lateral ventricles. It is responsible for a structural partition (two laminae) forming the medial wall of the frontal horns of the lateral ventricles.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Partition the ventricles.** Form the thin translucent wall between the anterior horns of the two lateral ventricles.
- **Provide a landmark.** Serve as a midline structural and radiological landmark; a fluid-filled gap between its laminae is the cavum septi pellucidi (a developmental variant).

## 3. Behavioural Description

Primarily structural; absence or a large cavum is associated with certain developmental conditions but it has no processing role.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a passive structural partition, not a processor.
