# Region Summary: Left Cerebral Hemisphere

**Region name:** left cerebral hemisphere
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** telencephalon
**Major components:** left frontal lobe, left parietal lobe, left temporal lobe, left occipital lobe, left limbic lobe, left insula, subcortex of left cerebral hemisphere, left lateral ventricle

---

## 1. Overview

The left cerebral hemisphere is the left half of the cerebrum — conscious perception, voluntary action, and cognition for the right side of the body. It is responsible for the left cortical lobes, left basal ganglia and white matter, controlling sensation and movement of the right body, plus cognition, memory, and emotion.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Perceive and act for the opposite body.** Process sensation from and command movement of the right side of the body (the motor/sensory pathways cross).
- **Run cognition.** Carry out perception, attention, planning, decision-making, and language processing across its lobes.
- **Hold memory and emotion.** Support declarative memory (temporal/limbic) and emotional processing (limbic, insula).
- **Coordinate via subcortex.** Use its basal ganglia to select and gate movement and habits.

## 3. Behavioural Description

The left hemisphere governs the right body and, by lateralisation, most language. Damage causes right-sided weakness/sensory loss and aphasia.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a full cortical+subcortical processor for one side: route the relevant lobe sub-agents and integrate their outputs into perception, decision, and action.
