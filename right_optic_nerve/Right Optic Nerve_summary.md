# Region Summary: Right Optic Nerve

**Region name:** right optic nerve
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** diencephalon
**Latin / meaning:** "cranial nerve II"
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The right optic nerve is the right visual input cable carrying retinal output from the right eye toward the brain. It is responsible for transmission of visual signals from the right retina to the optic chiasm, preserving the retinotopic map.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Carry retinal output.** Convey the axons of right-retinal ganglion cells (a CNS white-matter tract, myelinated by oligodendrocytes) toward the optic chiasm.
- **Preserve the visual map.** Maintain retinotopic order so downstream targets can reconstruct the visual field.
- **Serve the light reflex.** Provide the afferent limb of the pupillary light reflex.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage to the right optic nerve causes vision loss in the right eye and an afferent pupillary defect; it does not, by itself, cause a field cut respecting the vertical meridian (that begins at the chiasm).

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a faithful input cable: it transmits and preserves the visual signal but does not interpret it.
