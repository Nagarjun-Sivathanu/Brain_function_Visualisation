# Region Summary: Right Hemisphere of Cerebellum

**Region name:** right hemisphere of cerebellum
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** cerebellum
**Major components:** lateral hemispheric zones, intermediate zone, dentate nucleus, cerebellar white matter

---

## 1. Overview

The right hemisphere of cerebellum is the right lateral cerebellum coordinating skilled right-sided limb movement and motor planning. It is responsible for coordination, timing, and learning of right-sided (ipsilateral) limb movement, and planning support via the dentate–thalamo–cortical loop.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Coordinate ipsilateral limbs.** Smooth and time right-sided limb movements, preventing dysmetria and intention tremor on the right side.
- **Plan and time movement.** Through the dentate nucleus, contribute to the planning, initiation, and timing of voluntary movement via thalamus and motor cortex.
- **Learn and automate skills.** Adapt limb calibrations with practice so movements become accurate and automatic.

## 3. Behavioural Description

Damage to the right cerebellar hemisphere causes ipsilateral (right-sided) limb ataxia, dysmetria, intention tremor, and dysdiadochokinesia.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as the right-limb coordination module of the cerebellar comparator, feeding corrected timing/scaling back to cortex.
