# Region Summary: Left Lateral Ventricle

**Region name:** left lateral ventricle
**Hierarchy level:** 5
**Parent:** left cerebral hemisphere
**Major components:** anterior (frontal) horn, body, temporal horn, occipital horn, choroid plexus

---

## 1. Overview

The left lateral ventricle is the large CSF cavity within the left cerebral hemisphere. It is responsible for cerebrospinal-fluid production and storage within the hemisphere.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Produce and hold CSF.** Its choroid plexus secretes cerebrospinal fluid that cushions and nourishes the brain; the C-shaped cavity stores and routes it toward the third ventricle.
- **Cushion and clear.** Contribute to mechanical protection, buoyancy, and metabolic waste clearance.

## 3. Behavioural Description

A fluid space with no neural function; enlargement (ventriculomegaly/hydrocephalus) reflects CSF imbalance or tissue loss and raises intracranial pressure.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as a passive hydraulic reservoir supporting the hemisphere.
