# Region Summary: Brachia of Right Inferior Colliculus

**Region name:** brachia of right inferior colliculus
**Hierarchy level:** 4
**Parent:** right side of midbrain
**Major components:** —

---

## 1. Overview

The brachia of right inferior colliculus is the right white-matter arm carrying auditory fibres from the inferior colliculus to the thalamus. It is responsible for conduction of right auditory fibres from the inferior colliculus to the medial geniculate nucleus.

## 2. Detailed Functional Description

- **Relay hearing onward.** Carry organised auditory signals from the inferior colliculus to the medial geniculate body for onward projection to auditory cortex.

## 3. Behavioural Description

As a fibre bundle it has no standalone function; damage interrupts the ascending auditory relay to the thalamus.

## 4. Functional Mimicry Notes (for agent modelling)

Model as an auditory output cable from the midbrain to the thalamus.
